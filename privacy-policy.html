This Privacy Policy explains how Lighting Analyzer (the “App”) and its developer access, collect, use, retain, and share your information. “We” refers to the developer or operator identified in the store listing from which you obtained the App. For privacy questions and rights requests, see Section 13. This policy covers the App. Information independently processed by third-party providers is also subject to their own privacy policies.

These services may process location or search information, device and network information, advertising identifiers, and purchase information. Exported or shared reports may reveal a residential location, floor, or window position. Choose storage destinations and recipients carefully.

1. Scope and App functions

The App estimates direct sunlight and generates reports using the building footprints, heights, obstructions, floor, window position, time zone, and analysis dates you draw or enter. The current version has no App accounts, developer-operated project cloud synchronization, or cloud report recovery service. We have not separately integrated a developer-operated user analytics or crash-reporting platform. Third-party SDKs may nevertheless collect their own operational, diagnostic, and service usage data.

We do not require your name, identity documents, phone number, contacts, photo library, microphone recordings, or camera content. Do not enter unnecessary identifying information, exact apartment numbers, confidential plans, or information you are not authorized to use in project names, searches, models, or feedback. Coordinates combined with a floor and window position can identify a home and should be treated as sensitive personal information.

3. Permissions and optional features

  • Approximate/precise location: Requested when you actively use the location feature. Once authorized, entering the modeling screen may also read the last known location to display a location marker. You may allow only approximate location, deny permission, or revoke it in system settings. You can still pan the map, search for places, and draw manually.
  • Internet and network/Wi-Fi status: Used for maps, searches, ads, purchases, time checks, and network availability checks. These generally do not require a runtime permission prompt. Some online features are unavailable offline; saved information and local calculations remain usable, subject to the App's on-screen conditions.
  • Advertising identifiers: The advertising SDK may declare and use advertising ID and advertising service capabilities allowed by the system. You can manage these through Android advertising privacy settings. These are separate from location permission.
  • File access: Report import/export uses the Android system file picker and accesses the file or destination you select, without requesting all-files management permission. Choosing cloud storage or another file provider may upload the file to that service.

Denying location or choosing not to purchase does not prevent access to unrelated features. Withdrawing permission does not affect processing lawfully carried out beforehand. Revoking location permission alone does not remove building coordinates already saved in projects.

4. Recipients and third-party services

Sharing includes information sent directly by the App, information sent directly by integrated SDKs, and transfers you choose. We do not publicly publish your projects or sell complete reports to data brokers. The third-party processing described below does occur, so the App should not be understood as never sharing data.

4.1 Map and place search providers

  • Google LLC and its relevant service-providing affiliates: The Google Maps SDK supplies Google basemaps and processes map areas/interactions, network and device metadata, SDK identifiers, and SDK fault data. Place searches use Android Geocoder, which passes search content to the geocoding service configured on the device. On Google-enabled devices this is commonly Google, but it may instead be a device manufacturer or its location service partner. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps SDK data disclosures.
  • AMap Open Platform providers (AMap and its relevant service-providing affiliates): The App integrates AMap's Android 2D Map SDK and Search SDK. When you select AMap, load its maps, or search, these process necessary map requests, search terms, and App/device/system/network information. AMap's published map and search SDK disclosures also describe carrier, screen, signal, sensor, and optional OAID categories, depending on features and permissions; not every category is collected on every device or use. See the AMap Open Platform Privacy Policy. AMap independently processes its service data under its policy. The App does not invoke AMap route navigation or trajectory recording.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

The App may show banner ads and offer rewarded ads that you choose to watch. Google UMP obtains and updates advertising consent status. The App initializes and requests ads only when UMP indicates that ads may be requested. Whether a consent form, particular choices, or a partner list is displayed depends on your region, Google's determination, and publisher configuration. Accepting this policy is not treated as consent to all personalized advertising.

When the platform requires a way to revisit choices, you can use the App's “Privacy options” entry. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID and adjust advertising privacy settings in Android, and manage applicable preferences in Google My Ad Center. Non-personalized or limited ads may still process necessary IP, contextual, frequency-control, measurement, and fraud-prevention information.

An ad-free purchase stops in-App ad display, but does not disable all third-party communication. Purchase checks, maps, searches, and time checks may continue. The current implementation may also continue consent updates, advertising SDK initialization, or rewarded-ad preloading. We therefore do not promise that buying ad-free access completely stops advertising SDK data processing.

6. Purchases and report backups

After an annual report purchase through Google Play, the App records the unlocked fixed model locally. You may change the permitted floor, window position, and report year for that model and regenerate reports for free. These records include the model location and report content. Ad-free receipts and annual purchase records are used to verify entitlements, complete transactions, and prevent duplicate grants, not for ad targeting.

You can export completed annual reports as a JSON backup from the saved reports screen and import it using the file picker. Backups include completed reports and their model snapshots, including coordinates, floor, and window details. They do not include purchase tokens and do not restore model unlocks, free-regeneration rights, unused purchase credits, or unfinished tasks. Backups are ordinary readable files and are not encrypted by the App. Save them only in trusted locations with suitable access protection and share them carefully.

The developer has no cloud project/report library. Recovery of local information after uninstalling, clearing App data, device damage, or changing devices is not guaranteed. Google Play order records and restorable purchase status are independent of local files. Deleting local files does not cancel orders or automatically issue refunds.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Projects: Kept on your device until you delete them in the App, clear App data, or uninstall. Deleting a project does not delete independent model snapshots in saved annual reports.
  • Annual reports and annual entitlements: Kept in private device storage. The current version has no individual report deletion option. To remove all local data, use Android Settings → Apps → Lighting Analyzer → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App. This also removes other local projects, purchase credentials, credits, and settings. Consider backups carefully before doing so; their read-only restoration limits are explained in Section 6.
  • Ad-free receipts, quotas, settings, consent, and time caches: Kept until their state is updated, records are replaced, or App data is cleared/the App is uninstalled. If a successful Google Play synchronization shows the ad-free product is no longer owned, the App clears its local ad-free credentials. Verified local cached ownership may continue to be used when network checks fail.
  • Exports and shared copies: Kept by your selected storage destination or recipient. Clearing or uninstalling the App does not automatically delete them. Delete copies separately in file managers, cloud storage, email, or recipient services. Manufacturer-specific migration behavior may vary; uninstalling is not a substitute for checking all external copies.
  • Inquiries and feedback sent to us: Kept only as necessary to handle your request, carry out necessary follow-up, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations. We delete or anonymize it once the purpose is complete and retention is no longer legally required. You may request deletion at any time. Any information that must legally be retained will be restricted to the relevant purpose.
  • Information retained by service providers: Advertising, map, payment, and time service providers retain server logs, orders, and other records under their own policies and legal obligations. Clearing the App does not delete those records. Use their privacy request channels; you may also contact us for clarification.

The App is configured to disable standard Android backup, but this does not guarantee that every manufacturer's device migration, your own backups, or copying by someone with device access is prevented.

8. Security

We use Android private App storage and system permission isolation to protect local information and verify purchase receipt signatures. Time checks use HTTPS. Google discloses that Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Other third-party transmissions rely on their SDK and service security mechanisms. We do not ask for payment passwords or include purchase tokens in report backups.

Local projects, reports, and exported JSON do not have additional App-level encryption. Unlocked or rooted devices, malware, insecure cloud storage, or voluntary sharing can increase exposure. Use a device lock, keep your system updated, and grant other apps permissions carefully. No system is absolutely secure. If an incident affects information for which we are responsible, we will take remedial steps and notify users and authorities as required by applicable law.

We will respond within applicable legal time limits and verify only what is necessary to handle your request. We do not require Google passwords, payment passwords, or unrelated identity documents.

  3. Delete local information as described in Section 7, export completed reports as described in Section 6, and manage external backups separately.

The App has no account registration, so there is no App account to close. Google and other service accounts are managed by their respective providers. Withdrawing permission or deleting local information does not automatically cancel past transactions.

Users below the age at which they can make valid privacy or purchase decisions in their jurisdiction should use the App with guardian guidance and any legally required consent. Do not make unauthorized purchases or submit other people's residential information. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information that we should not collect, contact us; after verification, we will delete it or take other steps required by law. Third-party accounts, ads, and payments are also subject to those services' age rules.
